Plates

    How it is made

    Narration is synthesised locally with Kokoro (82M, q8 ONNX). Word timings are anchored to the measured speech window of each take, and the plates animate against those times.

    Every frame is drawn with Skia from a pure function of time, blended from three sub-frames for motion blur, and piped into ffmpeg as raw RGBA.
